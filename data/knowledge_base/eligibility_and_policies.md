# Darwix Loan Qualification Policies and Business Rules

## Section 1: Core Underwriting Eligibility Thresholds
- **Policy ID**: `darwix-core-eligibility`
- **Minimum Time in Business**:
  - Minimum of 6 months active operations required for short-term working capital and equipment leasing.
  - Minimum of 12 to 24 months active operations required for prime commercial term loans and revolving lines of credit.
  - Startups with less than 6 months of operations cannot qualify for standard term loans, but may be referred to personal credit lines or equipment vendor programs with an experienced co-signer.
- **Minimum Monthly Gross Revenue**:
  - $10,000 per month ($120,000 annualized gross business revenue).
  - Revenue must be verified through business bank accounts showing consistent active deposits. Cash-only unverifiable revenue cannot be counted.
- **Credit Score Guidelines**:
  - Initial qualification and inquiry requires only a **soft credit check**, which never affects the borrower's personal credit score.
  - Prime Commercial Loans: 660+ FICO preferred.
  - Standard Commercial Lines: 600+ FICO.
  - Alternative / Second-Chance Programs: 550+ FICO considered if business cash flow is consistently strong.
- **Eligible Business Entity Types**:
  - Limited Liability Companies (LLC).
  - C-Corporations (Inc.) and S-Corporations.
  - General Partnerships and Limited Partnerships.
  - Sole Proprietorships (must have a dedicated business checking account and registered DBA).

## Section 2: Location and Geographic Coverage
- **Policy ID**: `darwix-geographic-coverage`
- **Supported Locations**: All 50 United States and the District of Columbia.
- **Restrictions**:
  - Businesses registered or operating outside the United States are strictly ineligible.
  - U.S. Territories (e.g. Puerto Rico, Guam) are handled on a case-by-case basis through SBA programs.

## Section 3: Ineligible and Restricted Industries
- **Policy ID**: `darwix-restricted-industries`
- Darwix cannot fund businesses engaged in:
  - Gambling, casinos, or sports betting operations.
  - Adult entertainment or adult novelty venues.
  - Marijuana or cannabis cultivation and dispensaries (due to federal banking regulations).
  - Predatory lending or cash checking institutions.
  - Speculative real estate flipping or pyramid marketing schemes.

## Section 4: Maximum Loan Amount and Leverage Ratios
- **Policy ID**: `darwix-loan-limits`
- Unsecured commercial loans are capped at **2.5x to 3.5x average monthly revenue**. For example, a business generating $30,000/month can typically qualify for up to $75,000 to $105,000 in unsecured working capital.
- Loan requests exceeding 4x monthly revenue require substantial collateral, equipment equity, or SBA government guarantees.
