# Darwix Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

## FAQ 1: How fast is the funding process?
- **Doc ID**: `faq-funding-speed`
- **Question**: How quickly can I get funded, approved, and receive the funds in my bank account?
- **Answer**: Initial qualification is instant over the phone. Official underwriting approvals are provided within 2 to 4 hours of document submission. Once loan contracts are e-signed, funds are wired via same-day ACH or direct wire transfer in as little as 24 to 48 hours.

## FAQ 2: Does applying or checking my options affect my personal credit score?
- **Doc ID**: `faq-credit-impact`
- **Question**: Will applying or seeing what I qualify for hurt my credit score?
- **Answer**: No. Darwix performs only a soft credit pull during the qualification and quote generation phase. A soft credit inquiry has zero impact on your personal or business credit score. A hard inquiry is only performed with your explicit permission when you choose to finalize and accept an SBA or long-term bank contract.

## FAQ 3: Are there any upfront application or broker fees?
- **Doc ID**: `faq-fees-pricing`
- **Question**: Do you charge upfront fees or broker costs to apply?
- **Answer**: Absolutely not. Darwix never charges upfront application fees, review fees, or evaluation fees. Getting a pre-qualification quote is 100% free with no obligation to proceed. Any loan origination costs are transparently built into the loan amount and only deduct upon successful funding.

## FAQ 4: What documents do I need to submit to qualify?
- **Doc ID**: `faq-required-documents`
- **Question**: What paperwork or documentation is required?
- **Answer**: For loans under $150,000, our process is streamlined and paperless: all that is required is the last 3 to 6 months of business bank statements, a copy of the owner's driver's license, and a voided business check. For loans over $250,000 or SBA loans, the most recent 2 years of business and personal tax returns and year-to-date profit & loss statements are requested.

## FAQ 5: Are there prepayment penalties if I pay off the loan early?
- **Doc ID**: `faq-prepayment-penalty`
- **Question**: Can I pay off my loan early without penalty?
- **Answer**: Yes. All Darwix Commercial Term Loans and Revolving Lines of Credit have zero prepayment penalties. In fact, borrowers who pay off their loan early often receive a discount on remaining interest charges or fee rebates.

## FAQ 6: Can I qualify if I already have an existing business loan or Merchant Cash Advance (MCA)?
- **Doc ID**: `faq-existing-loans`
- **Question**: Can I get funding if I already owe money on another loan?
- **Answer**: Yes. You can still qualify. We offer second-position financing if your business cash flow comfortably supports additional debt service. We also specialize in debt consolidation and refinancing loans that pay off your existing expensive high-interest debts or MCAs to lower your monthly payments into a single lower-rate term loan.

## FAQ 7: Can startups with less than 6 months in business qualify?
- **Doc ID**: `faq-startups-under-6-months`
- **Question**: Do you lend to brand new startups under 6 months old?
- **Answer**: Standard commercial working capital loans require at least 6 months of operational revenue. For early-stage startups under 6 months, we offer specialized equipment financing where the machinery serves as collateral, or business credit card programs requiring a credit-worthy guarantor.
