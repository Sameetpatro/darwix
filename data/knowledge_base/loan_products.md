# Darwix Business Loan Products

## Product 1: Darwix Commercial Term Loan
- **Product ID**: `darwix-term-loan`
- **Loan Amount**: $25,000 to $500,000.
- **Terms**: 1 to 5 years (12 to 60 months).
- **Interest Rates**: Starting from 6.99% to 18.99% APR, depending on credit tier and business financial strength.
- **Repayment**: Fixed monthly or bi-weekly automated ACH debits.
- **Best For**: Business expansion, opening new locations, hiring staff, major equipment upgrades, or long-term growth initiatives.
- **Collateral**: Unsecured options available up to $150,000. General business lien (UCC-1) on business assets for larger amounts; no personal real estate pledge required.

## Product 2: Darwix Revolving Line of Credit
- **Product ID**: `darwix-line-of-credit`
- **Credit Limit**: $10,000 to $250,000.
- **Terms**: Revolving 12 to 24-month facility with annual renewal.
- **Interest Rates**: Starting from 8.50% APR.
- **Key Feature**: Draw capital on-demand 24/7. Pay interest only on the funds you draw, not the total facility amount. As you pay down the balance, credit availability replenishes automatically.
- **Best For**: Managing seasonal cash flow gaps, emergency expenses, meeting payroll during slow periods, or taking advantage of unexpected inventory discounts.

## Product 3: Darwix Equipment Financing
- **Product ID**: `darwix-equipment-financing`
- **Funding Amount**: Up to $1,000,000.
- **Terms**: 2 to 7 years (matching the useful economic life of the machinery).
- **Interest Rates**: Starting from 5.99% APR.
- **Key Feature**: Up to 100% of the equipment purchase cost covered (including delivery, installation, and setup).
- **Collateral**: The purchased equipment itself serves as the underlying collateral; no additional business assets needed.
- **Best For**: Heavy machinery, medical and dental devices, commercial vehicles, restaurant kitchen appliances, technology hardware, and manufacturing tooling.

## Product 4: Darwix SBA 7(a) & Express Loans
- **Product ID**: `darwix-sba-loans`
- **Loan Amount**: $50,000 to $5,000,000.
- **Terms**: Up to 10 years for working capital and equipment; up to 25 years for owner-occupied commercial real estate.
- **Interest Rates**: Wall Street Journal Prime + 2.25% to 2.75% (government-regulated maximums).
- **Key Feature**: Lowest cost of capital and longest repayment amortization in the market. Partially guaranteed by the U.S. Small Business Administration.
- **Best For**: Buying commercial real estate, major business acquisitions, partner buyouts, and large-scale debt refinancing.

## Product 5: Darwix Short-Term Working Capital
- **Product ID**: `darwix-working-capital`
- **Loan Amount**: $10,000 to $150,000.
- **Terms**: 3 to 18 months.
- **Speed**: Fastest program available — approval within 4 hours, funded in your bank account within 24 hours.
- **Repayment**: Small daily or weekly ACH debits tied to business cash flow.
- **Best For**: Urgent short-term cash crunches, bulk inventory purchases for seasonal peaks, bridge financing.
