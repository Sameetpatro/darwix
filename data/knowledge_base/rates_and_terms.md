# Darwix Interest Rates, Pricing, and Repayment Guidelines

## Section 1: Interest Rate Tiers and APR Range
- **Doc ID**: `rates-tier-structure`
- **APR Range**: 5.99% to 24.99% APR.
- **Tier 1 (Prime Commercial)**: 5.99% to 10.99% APR.
  - Criteria: 2+ years in business, $30k+/month revenue, 680+ credit score, profitable tax returns.
- **Tier 2 (Standard Commercial)**: 11.00% to 17.99% APR.
  - Criteria: 1+ year in business, $15k+/month revenue, 620+ credit score.
- **Tier 3 (Alternative / Working Capital)**: 18.00% to 24.99% APR.
  - Criteria: 6+ months in business, $10k+/month revenue, 550+ credit score.
- **Rate Discounts**:
  - 0.25% APR discount for automatic recurring ACH debits.
  - Repeat borrower loyalty discounts of 1.0% off origination fees.

## Section 2: Repayment Structures
- **Doc ID**: `repayment-methods`
- **Monthly Repayment**: Available for all standard term loans with terms exceeding 12 months.
- **Weekly / Bi-Weekly Repayment**: Common for shorter-term working capital (3 to 12 months) to avoid large lump-sum end-of-month cash drainage.
- **Payment Method**: Automatic ACH transfer directly from the borrower's designated commercial checking account.
