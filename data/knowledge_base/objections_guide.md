# Darwix Objection Handling & Scripting Guide

## Objection 1: "I am worried your interest rates are too high."
- **Doc ID**: `objection-high-interest`
- **Context**: Borrower expresses concern regarding predatory rates or excessive interest costs.
- **Official Guidance**: Explain that Darwix partners with top commercial institutional lenders to provide competitive risk-based pricing starting as low as 5.99% to 6.99% APR. Because our review uses a soft pull, we compare multiple competing offers without committing or harming credit.

## Objection 2: "I've been burned by high upfront fees in the past."
- **Doc ID**: `objection-broker-fees`
- **Context**: Borrower is hesitant due to prior bad experiences with dishonest loan brokers.
- **Official Guidance**: Reassure the borrower that Darwix has a strict $0 upfront fee guarantee. We never ask for bank account debit authorizations or payment prior to loan funding.

## Objection 3: "I don't have time for extensive paperwork or bank visits."
- **Doc ID**: `objection-no-time-paperwork`
- **Context**: Busy business owner doesn't want endless bureaucracy.
- **Official Guidance**: Emphasize our 100% digital, paperless workflow. All that is required is uploading 3 to 6 months of PDF bank statements, which can be done directly from a smartphone or computer in under five minutes.
