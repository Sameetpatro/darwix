# Darwix Customer FAQs and Policy Notes

## FAQ 1: Prepayment Penalties
Question: Are there prepayment penalties if a borrower settles the loan early?
Answer: Absolutely not. All Darwix Commercial Term Loans and Revolving Lines of Credit have zero prepayment penalties. Borrowers can pay off the full balance at any time and receive a discount on unaccrued interest charges.

## FAQ 2: Funding Timeline and Speed
Question: How fast can a commercial borrower receive funds?
Answer: Approvals are granted in 2 to 4 hours after submitting digital bank statements. Once loan contracts are electronically executed, funds are transferred via same-day wire or automated ACH in 24 to 48 hours.

## FAQ 3: Soft Credit Check Guarantee
Question: Does applying hurt the business owner credit score?
Answer: No. Darwix performs only a soft credit pull to generate loan offers and pre-qualifications. A soft inquiry has zero impact on credit scores.

## Objection 1: High Interest Rate Concern
Customer Context: The borrower believes rates are too high.
Guidance: Clarify that our rates start at 5.99% APR, which is significantly lower than alternative merchant cash advances (which can exceed 60% APR). We provide competitive bank-grade pricing based on cash flow strength.
