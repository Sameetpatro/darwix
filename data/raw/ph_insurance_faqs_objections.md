# Darwix Philippines Life Insurance — FAQs and Objection Handling Guide

## Section 1: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### FAQ 1: How does premium payment work and what happens if I miss a due date?
- **Answer**: Premiums can be paid monthly, quarterly, semi-annually, or annually via bank auto-debit (BDO, BPI, Metrobank, Security Bank), online banking, GCash, or Maya. Every policy includes a statutory 31-day grace period. If you miss a payment, your coverage remains 100% active during those 31 days. If unpaid after 31 days without cash value, the policy lapses, but you can reinstate it within three years.

### FAQ 2: What is the difference between Term Life and Whole Life insurance?
- **Answer**: Term Life (Darwix SecureTerm) provides pure financial protection for a specific period (e.g., 10, 20 years) with the lowest possible premiums, ideal for family protection or debt coverage. Whole Life (Darwix LifeHeritage) provides guaranteed lifetime protection up to age 100 and builds guaranteed cash values and policy dividends over time.

### FAQ 3: Can I name my children or parents as beneficiaries?
- **Answer**: Yes. Under the Philippine Insurance Code, you may designate your spouse, children, parents, siblings, or any person who has an insurable interest as your primary or contingent beneficiaries. Minors can also be named with an appointed trustee or guardian.

### FAQ 4: What riders can I attach to my life insurance policy?
- **Answer**: You can attach optional riders such as the Critical Illness Benefit (CIB covering 36 critical illnesses like cancer and heart attack), Accidental Death & Dismemberment (ADB), Waiver of Premium upon Disability (WP), and Daily Hospital Income Benefit (HIR).

### FAQ 5: What are the perks of getting a policy through Bancassurance bank referral?
- **Answer**: If you are referred by or hold an account with our partner banks (BDO, BPI, Metrobank, or Security Bank), you qualify for express non-medical underwriting up to ₱3,000,000 face amount, hassle-free automated auto-debit arrangement (ADA), and a 5% first-year premium rebate.

## Section 2: Localized Objection Handling

### Objection 1: "Masyadong mahal ang premium / The premium is too expensive"
- **Context**: The customer feels insurance is beyond their current budget.
- **Handling Approach (Taglish / English)**:
  - *"Naiintindihan ko po kayo. Pero ang kagandahan po sa Darwix, pwede po nating i-customize ang plan para swak sa inyong budget. Meron po tayong SecureTerm na nagsisimula lamang sa humigit-kumulang ₱1,500 kada buwan—o ₱50 lang bawat araw, kasing halaga lang ng isang merienda o kape. Gusto niyo po bang gawan natin ng quotation na pasok sa inyong buwanang budget?"*
  - *"I completely understand your concern. The great thing with Darwix is that we customize the policy to your specific budget. Our SecureTerm starts at around ₱1,500 per month—just about ₱50 a day, which is the cost of a daily cup of coffee or merienda. Would you like to review a plan customized to your monthly budget?"*

### Objection 2: "Baka malugi lang ako o scam / I might just lose money or it's a scam"
- **Context**: The customer worries about financial stability or insurance scams.
- **Handling Approach (Taglish / English)**:
  - *"Naiintindihan ko po ang inyong pag-iingat. Ang Darwix Life po ay 100% lisensyado at mahigpit na pinangangasiwaan ng Insurance Commission ng Pilipinas. Bukod dito, partner po namin ang mga nangungunang bangko sa bansa tulad ng BDO, BPI, at Metrobank. Lahat po ng benepisyo at coverage ay nakasulat sa legal na kontrata upang sigurado ang proteksyon ng inyong pamilya."*
  - *"I respect your caution. Darwix Life is fully licensed and strictly regulated by the Insurance Commission of the Philippines. Furthermore, we are official bancassurance partners with top Philippine banks like BDO, BPI, and Metrobank. Every single peso of death benefit and guaranteed cash value is legally secured by strict capital reserve requirements."*

### Objection 3: "Wala pa akong budget ngayon, next time na lang / No budget right now"
- **Context**: Customer wants to postpone.
- **Handling Approach (Taglish / English)**:
  - *"Opo, nauunawaan ko po. Pero alam niyo po ba na habang mas bata at malusog pa tayo, doon pinakamura ang premium ng life insurance? Kapag naghintay po tayo, baka magmahal ang rate o magkaroon ng health issues na magpapahirap sa pag-apply. Pwede po tayong magsimula sa pinakamababang coverage muna habang nag-iipon."*
  - *"I understand completely. However, securing a policy while you are young and in good health locks in the lowest possible premium for the entire term. Waiting often means higher premiums due to age or future health changes. We can start with a basic affordable protection plan today and upgrade it as your income grows."*
