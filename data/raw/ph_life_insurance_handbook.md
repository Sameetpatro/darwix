# Darwix Philippines Life Insurance & Bancassurance Underwriting Handbook

## 1. Program Overview and Policy Types
Darwix Philippines, in partnership with leading universal banks (BDO Unibank, Bank of the Philippine Islands (BPI), Metrobank, and Security Bank), offers tailored life insurance and bancassurance solutions regulated under the Insurance Commission of the Philippines (IC).

### Available Policy Categories:
1. **Darwix SecureTerm (Term Life Insurance)**:
   - Pure protection policy providing maximum financial security at affordable premium rates.
   - Coverage terms: 5 years, 10 years, 20 years, or term-to-age-65.
   - Face amount coverage: Minimum ₱500,000 up to ₱10,000,000.
   - Renewable and convertible to permanent life insurance without evidence of insurability before age 60.

2. **Darwix LifeHeritage (Whole Life Insurance)**:
   - Permanent life insurance providing lifetime coverage up to age 100 with guaranteed cash values and policy loan privileges.
   - Premium payment options: Regular Pay up to age 65, 10-Pay (fully paid in 10 years), or 20-Pay.
   - Face amount coverage: Minimum ₱1,000,000 up to ₱25,000,000.

3. **Darwix InvestShield (Variable Unit-Linked / VUL)**:
   - Hybrid protection and investment-linked life insurance. Premiums are allocated between life protection and professionally managed Philippine equity, bond, and balanced funds.
   - Face amount: Minimum 125% of single premium or 5x annual premium.

## 2. Premiums and Payment Schedules
Premiums are determined by entry age, smoking status, biological sex, health classification, and selected coverage amount.

### Standard Premium Guidelines:
- **Payment Modes**: Monthly (via Auto-Debit Arrangement / ADA or post-dated checks), Quarterly, Semi-Annual, and Annual.
- **Annual Premium Discount**: Policyholders selecting Annual payment receive an effective 8% discount compared to monthly installment equivalents.
- **Sample Premiums (30-year-old Non-Smoker, ₱1,000,000 Coverage)**:
  - SecureTerm (20-Year Term): ₱1,450 per month (₱16,000 annually).
  - LifeHeritage (20-Pay Whole Life): ₱3,200 per month (₱35,000 annually).
- **Grace Period**: All policies include a statutory 31-day grace period for payment of renewal premiums. The policy remains in full force during the grace period.

## 3. Policy Lapse and Reinstatement Rules
- **Lapse Condition**: If a premium is not settled within the 31-day grace period and there is insufficient cash value or automatic premium loan (APL) balance, the policy will lapse.
- **Reinstatement Window**: Lapsed policies can be reinstated within three (3) years from the date of default.
- **Reinstatement Requirements**:
  1. Submission of Application for Reinstatement with Health Declaration.
  2. Proof of insurability satisfactory to Darwix Underwriting.
  3. Settlement of all overdue premiums with compound interest at 6% per annum.
  4. Payment or reinstatement of any outstanding policy indebtedness.

---

## 4. Beneficiaries Designation and Rules
- **Primary vs Contingent Beneficiaries**:
  - Primary Beneficiaries: Receive proceeds upon the insured's demise.
  - Contingent Beneficiaries: Receive proceeds if all primary beneficiaries predecease the insured.
- **Revocable vs Irrevocable**:
  - Revocable: The policyholder can change beneficiaries at any time without their consent.
  - Irrevocable: The beneficiary's written consent is required for policy amendments, withdrawals, or beneficiary changes.
- **Eligible Beneficiaries**: Under Philippine law, beneficiaries must have an insurable interest (spouse, legitimate or illegitimate children, parents, siblings, or legal dependents).

## 5. Supplemental Riders (Add-On Benefits)
Policyholders can attach optional riders to enhance coverage:
1. **Accidental Death and Dismemberment (ADB)**:
   - Doubles the face payout if death occurs as a direct result of an accident.
   - Pays schedule of indemnities for loss of limbs, sight, or hearing.
2. **Critical Illness Benefit (CIB)**:
   - Lump-sum cash payout upon diagnosis of any of 36 covered critical illnesses (including stroke, cancer, heart attack, kidney failure, major organ transplant).
   - Additional rider premium starting at ₱450/month for ₱500,000 critical illness coverage.
3. **Waiver of Premium upon Disability (WP)**:
   - Waives all future policy premiums if the insured becomes totally and permanently disabled before age 60, while keeping the full death benefit active.
4. **Hospital Income Rider (HIR)**:
   - Daily cash allowance (₱1,000 to ₱5,000 per day) during hospital confinement for injury or sickness.
